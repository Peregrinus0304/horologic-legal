Privacy Policy

How TickLog handles information when you measure a mechanical watch.

Effective date: 20 September 2026

1. Who we are

TickLog (“we”, “us”) is an iOS app that listens to a mechanical watch through the microphone (or analyses a recording you choose) and estimates beat rate, daily rate, beat error, and amplitude. This policy explains what information is used and where it stays.

2. Information we process

  • Microphone audio — used only while Live analysis is running, to detect tick sounds. Audio is processed on your device. We do not upload recordings to a server, and we do not keep the raw microphone stream after the session ends.
  • Audio files you open — analysed locally on the device. TickLog does not send those files to us.
  • Watch and session library — names, calibre, measurements, and notes you save. Stored in the app’s local database on that device (SwiftData). Deleting the app deletes this library unless you restore an iPhone backup that includes it.
  • Preferences — lift angle, language, appearance, and similar settings, stored on device (UserDefaults).
  • Purchase status — whether TickLog Pro is unlocked. Billing is handled by Apple through StoreKit. We do not receive your payment card number. Entitlement follows your Apple ID via Restore.

TickLog does not create a TickLog account and does not sync your library to our servers.

3. How we use information

  • To measure the watch and show results in the app.
  • To keep your history, watches, trends, and PDF reports on that device.
  • To unlock Pro features after a verified App Store purchase or restore.

We do not use this information for advertising, do not sell it, and do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites. There is no App Tracking Transparency prompt because TickLog does not track in that sense.

4. Where information is stored

  • On your device — measurements, watches, and settings.
  • Apple — In-App Purchase and restore are processed by the App Store.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only as needed to run the App Store purchase (Apple) or when required by law.

6. Retention

Library data stays on the device until you delete a session/watch in the app or delete TickLog. We do not keep a cloud copy. Apple retains purchase records under Apple’s policies.

7. Your choices

  • Refuse or revoke microphone access in iOS Settings. Live analysis will not run without it; file analysis still works.
  • Delete sessions and watches inside the app.
  • Restore Pro on another device signed in with the same Apple ID: Settings → Restore Purchase, or the paywall Restore button.
  • Privacy questions: support@ticklog.app.

8. Children

TickLog is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. The effective date above will change when we do. Continued use of TickLog after an update means you accept the revised Policy.
